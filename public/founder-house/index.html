Latest milestones · product updates

Shipped since the last check-in

MCP server
Six tools live at /api/mcp — an AI agent can list markets, quote one, pay for it and read its full history. Streamable HTTP, no auth, no API key. The payment path is not reimplemented: the 402 an agent receives is the gateway's own challenge, passed through.
x402 settled
Cross-chain buys now settle with real funds — USDC paid on Base, tokens delivered by the curve on the destination chain, buyer never holds its gas token. Delivery runs before the charge, so a failed fill costs us and not the buyer.
Own indexer
Envio HyperIndex on Monad: full history from the factory's deploy block, 1.93M blocks in under 45 seconds, checked figure by figure against what the curve contracts store. Read-only GraphQL is public and anonymous.
Trading terminal
Nine timeframes from 1s to 1y with a full indicator set, live order book and trade feed — the piece that makes a minutes-old market actually tradable rather than just listed.

0G integration updates

Four layers of 0G, all in production

Not "deployed on 0G". Every launch touches three 0G services, and one of them from a market that trades on another chain.

0G Compute — the market agents
Every market's agent runs on the 0G router (glm-5.3), and launch artwork is generated there too. Each market is a permanent inference consumer, not a one-off call.
0G DA — launch metadata anchored
All three live markets have a real storage transaction on 0G. Including the market that trades on Monad — its metadata is anchored to 0G, so 0G carries provenance for markets it does not host.
0G Mainnet — factory + markets
Curve factory 0.11.0 live. Two of three markets are on 0G; the largest has 21 swaps and has burned 7.11 tokens through permissionless buybacks.
TDX attestation — read, not claimed
The router reports TDX via dstack for all three models we call, and /api/tee serves that declaration raw. We state that we read it and do not verify the quote ourselves.
ERC-8004 identity registry is present on 0G and binding is verified against ownerOf at launch. It records an identity — it does not start a strategy, and we do not claim otherwise.

Marketing campaigns · to launch after Founder House

Two campaigns, both settled on-chain

Credit Campaign — "Open your market on 0G, free"
0G credits cover the launch gas and a starter buyback, so a creator with an audience but no crypto can open a real market.
Anti-farm: one funded launch per wallet, and the credit is released only after the market records swaps from several distinct addresses. A dead market earns nothing, so the budget follows real activity rather than sign-ups.
Galxe Campaign — five quests, every one verifiable
No screenshots and no self-reporting. Each quest maps to an event a verifier can read off 0G:
Open a market · trade one · trigger a buyback — permissionless, and it burns supply · buy a market from another chain over x402 · bind an ERC-8004 identity you own.
The buyback quest is the one we would push hardest: anyone may call it, no key gates it, the funds cannot leave the contract, and every call permanently reduces supply. Engagement that costs the treasury nothing and leaves a burn on 0G behind it.
